# Hydropower Investigation Page: Why We Needed It, Where We Stand on Keywords, and How We're Fixing It

## Summary

We didn't build the hydropower investigation page just to make the website look nicer. We built it because of a real problem: people searching for hydropower geotechnical services were not finding NS Engineering easily enough.

Our research showed that NS Engineering already ranks well for general geotechnical and pile-testing searches. But we were not showing up clearly for a more specific and more valuable search:

> "Who does soil investigation for hydropower projects in Nepal?"

This matters because hydropower investigation work is high-value and very specific to Nepal's infrastructure needs. Some competitors, with clearer hydropower-focused content, were showing up ahead of us — especially on Perplexity, an AI search tool.

So the real issue wasn't that we lack the skills or experience. The issue was that our website content and keywords didn't clearly show our hydropower expertise in a way search engines and AI tools could easily find and trust.

---

## 1. How We Found This Problem

### 1.1 What the research showed

- NS Engineering shows up often for general geotechnical work, pile testing, and PDA testing.
- We show up for hydropower-related searches on Google AI Search, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, and Claude.
- But on Perplexity, when someone searched for hydropower investigation, NS Engineering did not appear.
- Instead, other companies like SILT Consultants, ICGS, Quartz Drilling and Lab, and Explorer Geophysical Consultants showed up.

This tells us something important: we're recognized for general geotechnical work, but not strongly enough for hydropower work specifically.

### 1.2 Why this matters for the business

This isn't just a small search-engine detail. It affects:

- how many potential clients find us,
- our visibility to hydropower consultants and project owners,
- how credible we look in the geotechnical investigation market,
- whether AI tools mention us when people ask about hydropower investigation in Nepal.

If a competitor shows up more often for hydropower searches, it means our website isn't presenting our hydropower work clearly enough.

### 1.3 The real cause

It wasn't a lack of skill. It was a gap in how we presented our work:

1. We were already strong in general geotechnical positioning.
2. We had good hydropower project experience, but it wasn't grouped together clearly as one topic.
3. Some project pages had strong technical details, but not always in a format AI tools could easily read and use.
4. We didn't have one dedicated page that clearly brought all our hydropower work together under the right keywords.

In short: what we do and how people search for it didn't match.

## 3. Where We Were Weak on Keywords

### 3.1 Hydropower authority was weaker than general geotechnical authority

Our biggest gap was in this keyword group:

- hydropower geotechnical investigation
- hydropower investigation Nepal
- soil investigation for hydropower projects in Nepal
- geotechnical investigation for hydropower projects

These are exactly the kind of specific searches that lead to real project opportunities — more valuable than general geotechnical terms.

### 3.2 No single hydropower "hub" page

We had hydropower project information, but it was scattered across many project pages instead of being pulled together on one clear page. This made it harder for AI search tools, which prefer one well-organized page over many scattered mentions.

### 3.3 Inconsistent project details

Some project pages had strong details, like:

- project capacity
- location
- testing methods
- drilling depth
- number of tests
- survey methods

But other pages were vague, just saying "geotechnical investigation" without real details. This made it harder for search tools to trust and use the information.

### 3.4 Not enough hard facts

AI tools and search engines like pages with clear, checkable facts:

- project names
- capacity in MW
- location
- methods used
- drilling depth
- number of tests
- standards followed
- project dates

Pages that just say things like "professional" or "trusted" don't help much, because those words can't be verified.

## 6. Our Fix

### 6.1 Build a dedicated hydropower investigation page

This was the main fix — one page that becomes the go-to authority on hydropower investigation in Nepal.

The page needed to:

- clearly state the topic: Hydropower Geotechnical Investigations in Nepal
- list verified hydropower projects
- show method types like drilling, rock mechanics, geophysical survey, and lab testing
- link to detailed project pages
- be organized and filterable

This is the kind of page AI search tools trust as a strong source.

### 6.2 Use facts, not marketing language

The page should rely on real, checkable facts, such as:

- project capacity in MW
- location
- investigation methods
- drilling depth (when known)
- geophysical methods used
- project year
- client name (if allowed to share)

Facts are more useful than general claims like "reliable" or "trusted."

### 6.3 Link everything together

The hydropower page should connect to:

- geotechnical investigation services
- drilling services
- rock mechanics services
- geophysical survey services
- lab testing services
- relevant project examples

This helps search engines and AI tools understand how everything relates.

### 6.4 Target the right keywords

We should clearly use phrases like:

- hydropower geotechnical investigation Nepal
- hydropower drilling Nepal
- hydropower rock mechanics Nepal
- hydropower geophysical survey Nepal
- hydropower project investigation
- soil investigation for hydropower projects in Nepal

These match what people are actually searching for, which makes them more valuable than general geotechnical phrases.

### 6.5 Connect services, projects, and the company clearly

We should structure our data so it's clear that:

- The company provides these services
- These services connect to hydropower project experience
- These projects prove our investigation capability

This helps AI tools give accurate answers when someone asks who does hydropower investigation in Nepal.
