# Goal 6: AI Search Visibility (GEO)

## Objective

The purpose of this test was to determine whether AI-powered search and retrieval systems can discover and accurately describe **N.S. Engineering & Geotechnical Services Pvt. Ltd. (NS Engineering)** for realistic prospective-client queries.

The test focused on category-level queries rather than only branded searches because category queries provide a better indication of whether NS Engineering is discoverable by a potential client who does not already know the company.

The four primary queries were:

1. "Who does pile testing in Nepal?"
2. "What is PDA testing and who provides it in Kathmandu?"
3. "Best geotechnical engineering firm in Nepal"
4. "Who does soil investigation for hydropower projects in Nepal?"

The intended test surfaces were:

1. ChatGPT with web search enabled
2. Perplexity with web search enabled
3. Claude with web search enabled
4. Google Search / AI Overview
5. Google AI Mode

At the time of this report, results have been collected for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Search/AI Overview.
---

# 1. Testing Methodology

## 1.1 Query selection

The queries were selected to represent realistic questions that potential NS Engineering clients might ask.

Three of the four queries are strongly category-oriented:

- pile testing in Nepal
- PDA testing in Kathmandu
- soil investigation for hydropower projects in Nepal

The fourth query asks for the "best" geotechnical engineering firm and therefore tests whether NS Engineering is considered among notable firms rather than simply whether its brand is retrieved.

No query explicitly included "NS Engineering", allowing the test to measure unprompted visibility.

## 1.2 Evaluation criteria

For every query/surface combination, the following were evaluated:

- Whether NS Engineering appeared at all
- Whether it appeared without being named in the query
- Whether the information was relevant to the question
- Whether the claims appeared consistent with NS Engineering's published information
- Which competing companies appeared when NS Engineering did not appear
- What type of information competitors appeared to have that could explain their visibility

AI-generated claims were not automatically treated as factual. Claims were considered candidates for verification against NS Engineering's website and other authoritative sources.

# 2. Query Results

## Query 1: "Who does pile testing in Nepal?"

### Google AI Search / AI Overview

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Google's answer listed N.S. Engineering & Geotechnical Services among companies providing pile-testing services in Nepal. It associated NS Engineering with pile testing including Pile Driving Analyzer (PDA) high-strain dynamic testing.

Other companies mentioned included Soil Engineering, I.C.G.S., Geo Engineering, AH Engineering Services, Sagarmatha Engineering Lab, Akara Materials Testing Laboratory, and Viswa Consult.

### ChatGPT

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

ChatGPT identified NS Engineering as a pile-testing provider and associated it with:

- PDA/high-strain dynamic testing
- PIT
- other pile-testing services
- major infrastructure work

### Claude

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Claude listed N.S. Engineering & Geotechnical Services first among firms involved in pile testing in Nepal. It associated the company with pile integrity and dynamic testing and referenced major infrastructure projects.

### Perplexity

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Perplexity lists NS Engineering as most visible pile-testing specialists in Nepal. which offers Pile Load Test, Dynamic Pile Load Test (PDA), Pile Integrity Test (PIT), Crosshole Sonic Logging (CSL) and Pile Lateral Load Test.

### Preliminary finding

NS Engineering demonstrated strong unprompted visibility for the pile-testing category on the available surfaces.

# 3. Query 2: "What is PDA testing and who provides it in Kathmandu?"

## Google AI Search / AI Overview

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Google described PDA as Pile Driving Analyzer / high-strain dynamic testing and identified NS Engineering as a provider in Kathmandu.

The response connected the company with PDA equipment, strain transducers, accelerometers, PDA reports, and capacity analysis.

It also referenced PDA work associated with the Kathmandu–Terai/Madhesh Fast Track.

## ChatGPT

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

ChatGPT identified N.S. Engineering & Geotechnical Services as a clear PDA provider in Kathmandu.

The response described PDA as high-strain dynamic testing and associated NS Engineering with PDA testing and major infrastructure work.

## Claude

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Claude identified NS Engineering as one of the firms providing PDA/dynamic pile testing in Nepal.

It described PDA as a dynamic method using instrumentation on a pile to measure force and velocity during hammer impact.

## Perplexity

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Perplexity identified:

- N.S. Engineering and Geotechnical Services Pvt. Ltd.
- AH Engineering Services Nepal

as the most visible PDA providers.

It specifically stated that NS Engineering offers Dynamic Pile Load Test/PDA together with static pile load tests, PIT, CSL, and lateral load tests.

## Preliminary finding

This was one of the strongest queries in the experiment.

NS Engineering appeared consistently across the available AI search surfaces for the relationship:

> PDA testing → NS Engineering

The result is particularly valuable because the query did not mention NS Engineering.

# 5. Query 4: "Who does soil investigation for hydropower projects in Nepal?"

## Google AI Search / AI Overview

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Google's result associated NS Engineering with hydropower geotechnical investigation and referenced projects including the Phukot Karnali PRoR HEP (480 MW).

It also surfaced other firms including ICGS, Explorer Geophysical Consultants, Innovative Engineering Services, Hydro Lab and Akara.

## ChatGPT

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

ChatGPT identified NS Engineering as one of the strongest matches for hydropower-related geotechnical investigation.

The response associated the company with several hydropower projects and with:

- drilling
- soil/rock laboratory testing
- in-situ testing
- geotechnical investigation

## Claude

**NS Engineering mentioned:** Yes.

Claude identified NS Engineering as an active firm for hydropower geotechnical investigation and associated it with deep drilling and an ISO-certified laboratory.

It also mentioned competitors such as ICGS, Explorer Geophysical Consultants, Quartz, SILT Consultants and others.

## Perplexity

**NS Engineering mentioned:** No.

This was the clearest negative visibility result.

Perplexity instead emphasized firms such as:

- SILT Consultants
- ICGS
- Quartz Drilling and Lab
- Explorer Geophysical Consultants
- Clean Energy Consultants
- Akara Material Testing Laboratory

Perplexity's response described SILT in particular as having strong hydropower-specific geotechnical investigation capabilities including:

- test pits
- core drilling
- laboratory testing
- slope stability
- foundation studies
- dam/intake/powerhouse/tunnel investigation

## Preliminary finding

This query exposed a meaningful cross-platform visibility difference.

NS Engineering appeared on Google AI Search, ChatGPT and Claude but was absent from the available Perplexity response.
